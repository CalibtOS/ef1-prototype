> Derived from `berat_proposal_deck.md` (19 slides). This is the **read-while-presenting** version — short chunks, pause/emphasis/eye-contact markers, natural spoken phrasing. Meaning is identical to the deck; only the *delivery* is optimized.
> If you change wording in the canonical MD, update this file too.

## How to read this
- Read **one line at a time**, top to bottom. Each line is one breath / one idea — never read two lines as one sentence.
- When you hit a `[ ]` marker, **do the thing, then continue.** The markers are instructions to *you*, never spoken.
- `**bold**` = lean on that word (say it a touch louder + slower). Don't rush past bold words.

## Marker legend
- `[PAUSE]` — a 1-second beat. Breathe.
- `[PAUSE 2s]` — a longer, deliberate hold.
- `[LET IT SINK IN]` — **stop**, look at Berat, hold ~2–3s. Let the slide land. Do not fill the silence.
- `[LOOK UP]` — eyes off the script, **look at Berat** while you say the line.
- `[EMPHASIZE]` — hit the next phrase harder and slower; it's a key line.
- `[SLOW]` — drop your pace for the next line.
- `[NEXT POINT]` — small beat, you're moving to the next idea on the *same* slide.
- `[→ CLICK]` — advance to the next slide.

## Before you start (pre-flight, from the rehearsal)
- **Go fullscreen before the call shows your screen** (F11 was flaky live — set it up first, confirm it's fullscreen, *then* share).
- Have the deck open in the browser; rail is hidden (`no-rail`), so use **← / →** or number keys to move.
- Two words to say cleanly (you slipped on these): it's "**police**" (not "polish") on Slide 10, and read each **number once** — don't repeat "look at this number… look at this number two."
- Total target: **~35–40 min**. If you feel rushed, cut words, not pauses.
